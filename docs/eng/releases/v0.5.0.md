# v0.5.0 — Priority-area contribution explanations

## Priority-area analysis

- Defined the 21 Busan administrative dongs in B-IMD decile 1 as priority areas.
- Decomposed each composite score into weighted contributions from six domains and nine
  indicators.
- Recorded the domain and indicator with the largest weighted excess above its citywide median
  as area-specific leading drivers.
- Automatically verifies that domain and indicator contributions reconcile to the published
  B-IMD composite score.

## Key results

- The three highest-ranked areas are Mora 3-dong in Sasang-gu, Choryang 6-dong in Dong-gu,
  and Banyeo 3-dong in Haeundae-gu.
- Employment is the leading domain for 12 of the 21 priority areas and income for nine.
- Reproducible processed outputs contain 21 priority-area profiles and 189 indicator-level
  contribution rows.

## Reproducibility and documentation

- Extended the network-free full rebuild through priority-area contribution analysis.
- Recorded input/output checksums, calculation definitions, and top-ten summaries in a tracked
  manifest.
- Added bilingual methodology covering execution, formulas, interpretation rules, and limits.
- Passed Ruff and all 101 tests.

## Limitations

- Priority status is relative within Busan, not an absolute poverty classification or statutory
  allocation rule.
- Contributions are arithmetic score decompositions, not causal or policy-effect estimates.
- The leading income and employment results rely respectively on inferred basic-livelihood and
  workplace-worker proxies and require validation against administrative data and field evidence.
- Mapping and the one-page infographic remain in the later COD-23 scope.
