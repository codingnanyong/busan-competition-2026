# v0.8.1 — Missing schedules and post-merge policy checks

## Transit scoring

- Dongs whose serving routes all lack usable BIMS schedule fields stay missing rather
  than scoring as zero supply.
- Rankings use observed schedules only. The 20% weight is omitted from the category
  sum, and the policy gate stays closed for that indicator.
- Custom `--route-stops-csv` parent directories are created, and the 2023 boarding
  validation digest covers boarding, current route-stops, and stop geometry.

## Repository automation

- After a pull request is merged, Linear follow-up edits no longer re-run the open-issue
  policy check. That re-run was the source of false failure alerts.

## Interpretation limits

- Results are an experimental relative screening of living conditions within Busan,
  not official statistics or a statutory funding rule.
