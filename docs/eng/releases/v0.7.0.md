# v0.7.0 — Public-data reliability and package structure

## Data reliability

- Combined 2025 student and teacher disclosures from Schoolinfo with existing NEIS school
  coordinates.
- Applied the joined records as evidence for educational supply within two kilometres of each
  administrative dong, with provenance and calculation conditions recorded.
- Collected current Busan bus routes, headways, and first and last service times separately so
  current-period validation data do not overwrite the 2025 primary score.

## Policy visualization

- Provides observed signals, priority groups, implementation steps, and monitoring indicators for
  each of the eight child categories.
- Links official policy examples from Busan and central government agencies to each category.
- Presents cases as design references requiring local validation, not ready-made policy answers.
- Shows whether estimated, proxy, or calibrated values were used, why they were needed, and their
  application limits in each dong detail view.

## Package structure and reproducibility

- Grouped index and policy modules under `analysis` and data preparation modules under
  `processing`.
- Split infographic configuration, profile calculation, rendering, and pipeline orchestration.
- Updated rebuild scripts, notebooks, bilingual documentation, and tests for the new paths.
- Passed Ruff, GitGuardian, and all 131 tests.

## Interpretation limits

- Results are an experimental relative screening of living conditions within Busan, not official
  statistics or a statutory funding rule.
- Estimated values and current-period validation sources require current administrative data and
  field review before policy use.
