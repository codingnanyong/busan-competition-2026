# B-IMD Expansion Model

## Stage 1 — Open-data model

Build a reproducible minimum viable model with consistent Busan-wide coverage, explicit proxy labels, domain drivers, confidence grades, versioned data, code, and execution environment. Every artifact labels the index as experimental.

## Stage 2 — Institution-supported model

Replace priority proxies with disclosure-controlled dong aggregates from Busan and partner institutions. Add direct income, employment, health, and safety measures; apply small-cell rules; and compare results with the open model.

## Stage 3 — Operational policy reference

Establish accountable data owners, regular refreshes, independent review, expert and community consultation, impact evaluation, external validity checks, misuse controls, appeals, and methodology revision.

| Transition | Minimum gate |
|---|---|
| Open → supported | Agreed legal basis, aggregation level, protection, and delivery for priority gaps |
| Supported → operational | Repeatable updates, independent review, policy validation, and an accountable owner |

Completing the open model does not grant the authority implied by later stages.
